# HACKATHON_PROBLEM_STATEMENT

> Markdown adaptation of the FlytBase BDR Hackathon problem statement.

## Overview

Build a working AI agent for outbound Business Development that accepts
a campaign brief and automatically produces:

-   Target accounts
-   Target contacts
-   Company research
-   Personalized outbound emails

The focus is on building a **working system**, not a slide deck or
prompt demo.

## Campaign Input

-   **Target Vertical:** Large-scale lithium, copper and iron ore mining
    operations in Latin America
-   **Reference Account:** Sociedad Química y Minera de Chile (SQM)
-   **Goal:** Book discovery calls with Heads of Operations, VP of HSE
    and Site Directors
-   **FlytBase Angle:** Autonomous drone inspection replacing contracted
    crews at hazardous 24/7 extraction sites.

## Required Pipeline

1.  Account Identification
2.  Contact Discovery
3.  Account Research
4.  Personalized Email Generation

## Requirements

-   Use real companies and contacts.
-   Research must be based on verifiable public information.
-   Emails must be personalized from research.
-   Agent must actually execute and produce output.
-   Failures should be visible and explained.

## Evaluation

-   Thought process (highest weight)
-   Research quality
-   Output quality
-   Communication

## Deliverables

-   Live deployed system
-   Submission.md
-   Mind map / Flowchart
-   5 minute walkthrough video

## Do

-   Use SQM as ICP anchor
-   Use real data
-   Show failures and recovery
-   Write human-quality emails

## Do Not

-   Fake outputs
-   Use placeholder templates
-   Submit only diagrams
-   Spend effort polishing slides instead of the agent
